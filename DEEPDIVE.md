# Bitmessage Deep Dive

A collection of technical resources and deep dives into the Bitmessage protocol and codebase.

## Technical Analysis

- [A Very Technical Look at BitMessage: Learning From a Dead Project](https://zola.ink/blog/posts/a-very-technical-look-at-bitmessage) by Zola Gonano
  - An excellent breakdown of the ECIES encryption scheme, Proof of Work (double SHA-512), and RIPEMD-160 address generation used in Bitmessage.
  - Discussion on potential improvements like HKDF and AEAD.
  - Detailed overview of the `keys.dat` and `messages.dat` file structures.
